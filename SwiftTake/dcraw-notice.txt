dcraw reference and attribution

SwiftTake's QuickTake 100/150 decompressors are Swift adaptations of
Dave Coffin's quicktake_100_load_raw and kodak_radc_load_raw routines.

The implementation was compared with dcraw 9.28, revision 1.478 (2018-06-01):
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ncruces/dcraw/master/dcraw.c
SHA-256: d18d9e43a096eea04eee2148e53068f8fa45ce95395d97128b1aa37b477eab43

The original upstream revision used during development is not yet established.
The matched QuickTake routines are outside the compared source's marked
restricted Foveon section. The notice below is reproduced from that source;
it does not license unrelated SwiftTake assets or other third-party materials.

SwiftTake changes include Swift data types, flat buffers, bounds checks and
truncated-payload handling. Its rendering and enhancement stages have also
been developed separately from the original dcraw rendering pipeline.
