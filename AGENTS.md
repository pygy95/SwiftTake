# SwiftTake contributor guidance

## Preserve the surprises

SwiftTake contains intentional Easter eggs. Keep their activation details out
of public-facing summaries, walkthroughs, release notes and ordinary help.
Do not reveal trigger sequences, special filenames, hidden commands or the
steps needed to unlock them. It is fine to mention that the app has surprises
and encourage people to discover them through use.

During explicitly requested maintenance or debugging of an Easter egg, discuss
only the details needed for that work. Preserve existing Easter eggs unless
the maintainer explicitly requests a change.

This is a spoiler courtesy, not a security boundary: the source remains
inspectable. Do not claim that Easter eggs are protected from discovery.
